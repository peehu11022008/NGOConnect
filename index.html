About NGOConnect

NGOConnect brings NGO discovery, volunteering, fundraising information and community awareness into one place, so it is easier to start contributing to causes you care about.

Awareness

Social service is more than donations. Giving a few hours, sharing a skill or spreading the word about an NGO also helps.

FAQ

How do I volunteer?

Open an NGO, choose a role under Volunteer Opportunities and select Apply. You need an account.

How does OTP login work?

Enter your email or phone, select Send OTP, then type the 6-digit code.

How do donations work?

Each NGO page shows how to contribute and the campaign progress.

I run an NGO. How do I join?

Create an account and choose NGO.
